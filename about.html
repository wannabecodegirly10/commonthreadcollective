How Booth Collective began

Booth Collective began as a partnership between two close friends who met at work nearly six years ago. Built on creativity, connection, and shared ambition, we create polished photo booth experiences that help people capture the moments and joy that make every event worth remembering.

Every detail — from the booth design to the final print — is crafted to feel as intentional and memorable as the celebration itself.

Based in Baton Rouge, creating memorable photo moments for the events that bring people together.
