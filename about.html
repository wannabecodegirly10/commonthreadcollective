Our Story

Rooted in friendship, built together.

Common Thread Collective began as a partnership between two close friends who met at work nearly six years ago. Built on creativity, connection, and shared ambition, we create polished photo booth experiences for the people and events we love.

Est.
Baton Rouge

Every detail — from the booth design to the final print — is crafted to feel as intentional and memorable as the celebration itself. We still stand with the guests. We still print on site. And we still decline nights that would make us rush.

What started as a weekend idea between friends is now a small studio serving weddings, brands, schools, nonprofits, and neighborhood milestones across Louisiana.

Based in Baton Rouge, creating memorable photo moments for the Gulf South.

How we work

Craft, hospitality, aftercare.

Craft

Studio lighting, real lenses, and print files we would put in a book. Props are optional. Taste is not.

Hospitality

Every booking includes a host. They keep the line kind, the backdrop clean, and your coordinator unbothered.

Aftercare

Online gallery within 48 hours, print extras on request, and a share link for every guest.
