FAQ

A few helpful details.

How do I reserve my date?

Your event date is reserved once your signed agreement and required retainer are received.

Do you offer custom branding?

Yes. We can customize photo overlays, welcome screens, event graphics, props, and full corporate activations.

Do you travel outside Baton Rouge?

Yes. We serve Baton Rouge and surrounding communities. Travel outside our standard service area may require an additional fee.

Can the booth be used outdoors?

Outdoor setups may require additional planning for weather, power, lighting, and booth placement. We will confirm those details with you before the event.

Can you create a prop I do not see listed?

Absolutely. Some of our favorite ideas begin with “Could you make…?” Share your concept and we will explore what is possible.
